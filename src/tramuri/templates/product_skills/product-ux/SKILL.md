# Product UX

## When to use
Shape the connected product journey and required surfaces after framing, buying
motivations and scope are sufficiently clear.

## When not to use
Do not create a marketing site, admin surface or extra screen unless the product
model or journey requires it. Do not present a design artifact as a production
implementation or validated UX.

## Inputs
Product scope, target users, key jobs, buying motivations, constraints and known
accessibility needs.

## Process
Define the primary journey, supporting surfaces, transitions and relevant
empty/error/success states. Explain why each surface exists and preserve explicit
product decisions and unresolved assumptions.

## Output contract
Return a journey map, required surfaces and states, accessibility considerations,
scope traceability and open validation needs.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Each surface serves a defined user or product need.
- [ ] The primary journey has a clear beginning and completion state.
- [ ] Important error, empty and success states are covered.
- [ ] No user testing or production readiness is claimed without evidence.
