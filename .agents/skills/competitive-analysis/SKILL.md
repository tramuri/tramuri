# Competitive Analysis

## When to use
Compare alternatives when competition or the status quo could change product
scope, positioning, or a build/buy/partner decision.

## When not to use
Do not create a competitor list just to fill a report or repeat market research
without a decision to inform.

## Inputs
Product framing, audience, research pack and the decision under consideration.

## Process
Compare relevant direct products, indirect alternatives and the status quo.
Capture positioning, observed capabilities, gaps and source IDs; distinguish
facts from inference and connect findings to differentiation or strategic tradeoffs.

## Output contract
Return the alternatives reviewed, comparison dimensions, source IDs, confidence,
differentiation hypotheses and decision implications.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every factual comparison has a source ID.
- [ ] Alternatives are relevant to the same user need.
- [ ] Inferences and unknowns are labeled.
- [ ] Build/buy/partner tradeoffs are included only when relevant.
