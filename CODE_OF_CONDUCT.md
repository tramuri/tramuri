# Code of Conduct

We want Tramuri to be a respectful and welcoming project for people with different backgrounds,
identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and work; do not attack people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or confidential data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and
publishing private information without consent are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub
contact channels. The maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality as far as
practical, and take action appropriate to the behavior. This may include a warning, removal of
content, or restriction from project spaces. Maintainers are expected to apply these rules fairly.
