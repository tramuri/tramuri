---
name: tramuri-hypotheses
description: Turn uncertainty into testable product hypotheses.
---

## When to use
Make a consequential uncertainty explicit and define how it could be tested.

## When not to use
Do not create hypotheses for confirmed information or invent validation results.

## Inputs
Problem, audience, value and scope decisions, evidence and unresolved risks.

## Process
State the belief, affected user or outcome, risk if false, smallest useful test
and observable success signal. Identify what evidence remains unknown.

## Output contract
Return hypothesis, rationale, risk, test method, success signal, evidence state
and owner or verification mechanism when known.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Each hypothesis is falsifiable and linked to a decision.
- [ ] Unobserved hypotheses remain unvalidated.
- [ ] Tests and outcomes are not claimed before they occur.
- [ ] Signals are observable without fabricated numeric targets.
