# Business discovery

Use only when the Core returns `business_discovery` from `tramuri mission define next`. Build on
accepted objective-context artifacts. Identify the problem, target audience, business objectives,
value proposition, business rules, uncertainties, and source references. Label unsupported audience,
buyer, market, pricing, and business-model statements as hypotheses. Do not claim customer or market
validation without direct evidence. Do available useful work before asking a material question.

Return the required structured JSON fields from the Core contract, plus explicit references and
provenance where available. Submit with `tramuri mission define submit`; do not mark a step complete
yourself. Structural validation is not evidence that a business hypothesis is true.
