# Product Brief

## Trigger

Use after framing, discovery and scope have enough information to explain the
product coherently to another person.

## Inputs

Product framing, discovery gaps, hypotheses, scope, user journeys, metrics,
decisions and risks from the current Tramuri run.

## Outputs

A concise, reviewable brief containing the problem, audience, value proposition,
scope, exclusions, assumptions, success criteria, delivery increment and open
validation work.

## Quality bar

Preserve provenance and classification for every claim. Separate fact, evidence,
inference, hypothesis, assumption, decision and pending validation. Do not make a
completed workflow sound like a validated business result.
