description: Critique assumptions, contradictions, scope and risks.
---

## When to use
Review a product definition or experience for contradictions, evidence gaps,
scope drift or material risks before delivery.

## When not to use
Do not use this review to invent a new product direction or silently rewrite
artifacts under review.

## Inputs
Current run artifacts, source references, decisions and validation receipts.

## Process
Check consistency between intent, audience, problem, value, scope, journeys,
research and success criteria. Report specific, material findings and give the
smallest useful repair recommendation.

## Output contract
Return findings with severity, affected artifact, supporting evidence or missing
evidence, rationale and repair recommendation. Return an explicit clean result
only when the reviewed scope was actually inspected.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Findings are specific and actionable.
- [ ] Evidence and inference are distinguished.
- [ ] No unsupported feature or market claim is introduced.
- [ ] Review is read-only; repair remains a separate workflow action.
