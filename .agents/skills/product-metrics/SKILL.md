description: Define observable outcomes and product success metrics.
---

## When to use
Define how a product outcome or experiment will be observed and evaluated.

## When not to use
Do not add vanity metrics or arbitrary targets where no decision depends on them.

## Inputs
Desired user or business outcomes, hypotheses, experiment context and available
measurement methods.

## Process
Connect each metric to an outcome and decision. Define measurement method,
baseline or target status, owner and timing only when known; otherwise state what
must be established.

## Output contract
For each metric return name, outcome, method, baseline/target state, owner or
verification mechanism and unresolved measurement needs.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Metrics map to outcomes or hypotheses.
- [ ] Unknown baselines and targets remain explicit.
- [ ] Collection method is understandable and feasible.
- [ ] No business result is implied by a proposed metric.
