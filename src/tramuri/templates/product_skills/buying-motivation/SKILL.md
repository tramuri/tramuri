# Buying Motivation

## When to use
Clarify why a target user might adopt, switch to, or pay for the proposed product.

## When not to use
Do not infer purchase intent from a feature request, market size, or one
unverified signal.

## Inputs
Target user, problem and value hypotheses, alternatives, research pack and
available evidence.

## Process
Identify desired outcomes, triggering events, switching costs, perceived risks,
urgency and objections. Separate sourced signals from hypotheses and relate each
to a product or positioning decision.

## Output contract
Return motivation, supporting source IDs or hypothesis status, confidence,
objections and implications for scope or positioning.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Motivations are tied to a defined audience and context.
- [ ] Evidence and hypotheses are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] Willingness to pay is not inferred from a single signal.
- [ ] No unsupported buying behavior is stated as fact.
