# Audience Sizing

## When to use
Estimate the addressable audience when size materially affects a product decision.

## When not to use
Do not estimate market size when the user only needs product framing or no
reliable source can support a useful estimate.

## Inputs
Product scope, target audience hypothesis, geography, time horizon and available
research sources.

## Process
Choose a defensible top-down or bottom-up method, explain the population and
filters, cite sources, and expose assumptions and uncertainty. Prefer a bounded
proxy to unsupported TAM/SAM/SOM precision.

## Output contract
Return the estimate or sizing model, method, geography, time window, source IDs,
assumptions, confidence and validation gaps. If evidence is inadequate, mark the
estimate pending validation.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every external number has a source and date.
- [ ] The method and population are reproducible.
- [ ] Assumptions and confidence are explicit.
- [ ] No fabricated precision or implied validation.
