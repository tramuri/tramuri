---
name: tramuri-prioritization
description: Prioritize product scope by user value, uncertainty and delivery risk.
---

## When to use
Choose between candidate capabilities when scope, sequencing or limited delivery
capacity requires an explicit tradeoff.

## When not to use
Do not prioritize a single item or create numerical scores without evidence or
decision criteria.

## Inputs
Candidate capabilities, user outcomes, product hypotheses, constraints,
dependencies and evidence.

## Process
Compare user value, uncertainty, risk, dependencies and effort only to the extent
supported by context. Explain tradeoffs; use qualitative ordering when numerical
inputs are unavailable.

## Output contract
Return in-scope, deferred and excluded items with rationale, dependencies,
evidence state and any required decision.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Criteria are explicit and relevant to the stated outcome.
- [ ] Dependencies and major risks are visible.
- [ ] Scores are not invented to imply precision.
- [ ] Every exclusion or deferral has a reason.
